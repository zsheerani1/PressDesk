PressDesk is a simple tool for small publishers. Upload a book manuscript and PressDesk splits it into chapters, so you can read it, keep notes, and record your decision, all from one dashboard.

{# ---------- How it works ---------- #}

How it works

  1. 1. Upload

    Drag a Word (.docx), text (.txt) or PDF file onto your dashboard and add its title, author and genre.

  2. 2. Read

    PressDesk finds the chapters for you. Read them one by one and mark each as read as you go.

  3. 3. Decide

    Add notes to any chapter, then move the manuscript through the stages below until you reach a decision.

{# ---------- Statuses ---------- #}

What the stages mean

Received
The manuscript has been uploaded but not opened yet.
Reading
You have started reading it. This happens automatically when you first open it.
Shortlisted
It is one of the strongest, and you are considering publishing it.
Accepted
You have decided to publish it.
Declined
You have decided not to publish it.
